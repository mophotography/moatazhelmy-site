About

Moataz Helmy is an Egyptian street and commercial photographer. His street photography, featured by CNN Arabic, shapes his approach to every project, capturing authentic moments with a strong sense of story and atmosphere.

Alongside editorial work, including a feature in Condé Nast Traveller Middle East, he photographs restaurants, hotels, clinics, private residences and high-end commercial spaces, as well as exclusive VIP and luxury events, and works with galleries and collectors. He is also a member of the Union of Arab Photographers (UAP).
