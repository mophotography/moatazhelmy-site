CURATION APPROACH
The selected set prioritizes establishing views of the restaurant, varied seating areas,
the long dining arrangement, distinct decorative features, and a small number of
branded/details photographs. Similar chair/table compositions and near-duplicate
framing variations were reduced. The kitchen/staff-window image was held out because
the brief is an interior portfolio rather than a staff or kitchen story. One of the
repeated bell views was retained as a single environmental detail, with near-duplicate
angles held out.
