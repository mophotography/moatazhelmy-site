Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This page explains how Moataz Helmy Photography measures visits to moatazhelmy.com and how you can manage your analytics choice.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors explore the portfolio and which sections are useful. Depending on your consent choice, measurement may include:

  • Page views and the pages or portfolio sections visited.
  • Clicks on navigation links, commercial client pages, gallery images and calls to action.
  • Clicks on print enquiries and contact channels such as WhatsApp, email and Instagram.
  • Technical and referral information used by Google Analytics to report and estimate site usage.

The custom interaction events on this site are designed to report the type of action, the relevant portfolio section and a limited label where useful. They do not intentionally include the contents of private messages or details typed into forms.

Your analytics choice

If you select Accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics may use analytics storage such as first-party cookies for fuller measurement. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization remain denied by this site's tag configuration.

If you select Reject analytics cookies, analytics storage remains denied and Google Analytics will not read or write Analytics cookies while that state applies. This site uses Google's Advanced Consent Mode, so cookieless page and interaction signals are still sent to Google. Depending on the action, a custom event may describe the kind of click, the page or portfolio section, a commercial client gallery, a print title, or a contact channel. Our custom events omit URL query strings and private message contents. Google Analytics' separate Enhanced Measurement settings may also collect outbound link details, including the linked URL, if outbound-click measurement is enabled in the Analytics property. These signals and browser request information may be used for aggregated measurement and modelling, but they do not provide a complete, individually attributed record of every rejected visitor's clicks. Google documents that cookieless pings may include browser user-agent, screen resolution and IP address; Google Analytics states that it does not store or log IP addresses.

Cookieless measurement is still a transfer of information to Google. Rejecting Analytics cookies does not mean that no measurement signals are sent.

Changing your choice

You can reopen the notice at any time using Privacy choices in the footer of any page. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember it on later visits in that browser. Clearing browser storage or using another browser or device may reset the choice.

Third-party services

Google Analytics is provided by Google and processes measurement signals according to Google's terms and privacy information. This site also links to external services, including WhatsApp and Instagram. When you follow those links, the relevant service's own privacy terms apply.

Contact

For questions about this notice or the site's analytics settings, contact info@moatazhelmy.com.
